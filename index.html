👩‍🍳 ABOUT BAKETERIA

Our Story & Passion for Baking

Baketeria started with a simple belief: every sweet moment deserves a cake that tastes like pure home and looks like a work of art.

What began as passion baking in a home kitchen has grown into a beloved neighborhood bakery. Our bakers carefully select every egg, block of butter, and fresh fruit slice to ensure an unforgettable moist texture and heavenly aroma.
